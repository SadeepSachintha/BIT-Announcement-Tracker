#### Content & Details

#### IMPORTANT NOTICE TO STUDENTS

#### INSTRUCTIONS FOR MARKING MCQ ANSWER SHEETS

It has been observed that certain errors made by students while marking MCQ answer sheets have adversely affected the accuracy of results obtained through machine scanning.

To address this issue, an instructional video has been prepared in three languages and uploaded to guide students on the correct method of marking MCQ answer sheets and to help avoid such errors.

All students are kindly requested to carefully review the relevant video and strictly adhere to the instructions when marking answers in MCQ question papers.

Please note: MCQ answer sheets are evaluated purely through machine marking. Under no circumstances will manual marking be carried out for the current semester or any future examinations.
